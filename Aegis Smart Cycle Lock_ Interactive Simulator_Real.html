Questions

Likely questions with short, honest answers. Tap one to open it.

Why split the lock into two hubs?

The handlebar hub is exposed to thieves, so it only handles the interface (card reader, button, LED). The decision-making and the motor live in the armored rear hub, so attacking the handlebar never gives access to the lock itself.

What happens if someone cuts the cable?

A 3.3 V continuity loop runs through the cable and around the handlebar PCB. When it opens, the ESP32 sounds the siren. The bolt stays locked because the motor and the logic are in the rear hub, so cutting the cable is itself the alarm.

How does it stay low-power?

The ESP32 sits in deep sleep (about 10 µA for the module) and is woken only by the MPU6050 motion interrupt on GPIO 25. The Power budget section above shows the runtime estimate and the assumptions behind it, including extra draw from other parts.

Can the NFC card be cloned?

If the system only checks the card’s UID, yes, because UIDs can be copied. The planned fix is DESFire cards with AES challenge-response, where the secret key proves identity instead of the ID.

Is the UART unlock command secure?

Not fully. It is a plain-text command between the hubs, so it could be replayed by someone who taps the cable. Adding an HMAC or rolling code is the proposed fix, and the tamper loop makes physical tapping risky.

Why a worm-gear motor and a buck converter?

A worm gear gives high torque at low speed and is self-locking, so forcing the bolt can’t spin the motor backwards. A buck converter drops 12 V to 3.3 V efficiently, while an LDO would waste most of that energy as heat.

Can the bike still be lifted and carried away?

Yes, the lock only immobilizes the rear wheel. The siren and accelerometer alert people if it is lifted hard, and the planned chain port would tie it to a fixed pole.

What if the battery dies while locked?

The bolt stays locked, and USB-C charging recovers the pack. A mechanical override is not part of the current design, so it is listed as a future improvement.
